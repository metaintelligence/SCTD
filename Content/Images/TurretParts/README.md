# SCTD Turret Parts

Modular turret concept parts for 3D generation and in-game assembly.

The gameplay-facing turret composition is intentionally limited to three part categories:

- Base / Body
- Weapon
- AI Core

AI Core parts are internal components and do not require standalone turret-shape render images in this batch.

## Tower Type

Vertical launcher style, similar to a rocket silo or upward-firing mortar.
The newer Tower variants use an industrial yellow/black hazard palette and taller utility-pylon silhouettes for quick visual separation.

- Base: `SiloLift Tower Body`
- Base: `TripodRail Tower Body`
- Base: `MetroPylon Tower Body`
- Weapon: `Skyburst Rocket Pod`
- Weapon: `ArcMortar Plasma Tube`
- Weapon: `IonRain Launcher Array`

## Armor Type

Humanoid-armored style split into torso bodies and complete arm weapon modules.
The newer Armor variants use oxidized red armor and amber shock lights with bulky rounded exosuit silhouettes.

- Base: `Guardian Torso Body` - torso-only body with empty shoulder sockets.
- Base: `Paladin Torso Body` - heavier torso-only body with empty shoulder sockets.
- Base: `Bulwark Reclaimer Body` - reinforced torso-only body with heavy salvage armor and empty shoulder sockets.
- Weapon: `TwinBreaker Gauntlets` - complete left/right arm modules with crusher fists.
- Weapon: `EmberClaw Projectors` - complete left/right arm modules with heat projector claws.
- Weapon: `VoltHammer Manipulator Arms` - complete left/right arm modules with heavy shock-hammer fists.

## Cannon Type

Horizontal firing platform with stock/cradle-like body and swappable barrel modules.
The newer Cannon variants use deep navy armor and bright blue rail/coil lights with long, low artillery silhouettes.

- Base: `RailCradle Cannon Body`
- Base: `Bastion Stock Cannon Body`
- Base: `AnchorTrack Cannon Body`
- Weapon: `Longrail Piercer Barrel`
- Weapon: `HeavyBurst Cannon Barrel`
- Weapon: `CoilHowitzer Barrel`

## Notes

- Each part is generated as an isolated low-poly/cartoon cel-shaded 3D concept render on a dark studio background.
- Base images emphasize connector sockets.
- Weapon images emphasize plug/mount points.
- `Bastion Stock Cannon Body` is slightly closer to a complete cannon silhouette than the other base parts, but remains usable as a stock/cradle body concept.
